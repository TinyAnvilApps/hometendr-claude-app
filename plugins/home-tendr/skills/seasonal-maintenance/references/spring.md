# Spring home maintenance checklist

Source: https://hometendr.com/guides/spring-home-maintenance-checklist (keep in sync with hometendr-website/content/guides/spring-home-maintenance-checklist/en.php).

## Check for winter damage

- Roof and flashing: look for tiles that have moved, cracked or blown off, and check around the chimney and roof vents.
- Gutters and downpipes: ice can bend brackets and pull joints apart. Clean out what the winter left behind.
- Facade and foundation: cracks, flaking paint and damp patches near the ground are signs water is getting where it shouldn't.
- Windows: check the putty and paint on wooden frames, and clean the drainage holes in the bottom of the frames.

## Water and ventilation

- Turn outdoor taps back on and check the pipe inside the wall for leaks the first time you use them.
- Clean ventilation filters and air vents after a winter of closed windows.
- Clean the outdoor unit of an air source heat pump — remove leaves and dirt so air can pass freely.
- Test the smoke alarms. Many people do it when the clocks change, twice a year.

## Deck, garden and outdoor spaces

- Wash the deck and check the boards for rot and loose screws; oil or stain it when the wood is dry.
- Check fences and gates for posts that have moved with the frost.
- Service the lawn mower: sharpen the blade, change the oil and spark plug if it has a petrol engine.
- Clean garden furniture and check the barbecue — gas hoses and connections in particular.
