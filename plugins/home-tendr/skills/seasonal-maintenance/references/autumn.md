# Autumn home maintenance checklist

Source: https://hometendr.com/guides/autumn-home-maintenance-checklist (keep in sync with hometendr-website/content/guides/autumn-home-maintenance-checklist/en.php).

## Outside the house

- Clean gutters and downpipes once most leaves have fallen, and check that water runs away from the foundation.
- Look over the roof from the ground or with binoculars: loose or broken tiles, damaged flashing around the chimney, moss build-up.
- Shut off and drain outdoor taps before the first frost, and bring hoses inside. Water left in the pipe can freeze and split it.
- Clear drains and gullies of leaves so heavy autumn rain has somewhere to go.
- Store garden furniture, cushions and the lawn mower — empty or stabilise the fuel in a petrol mower before winter.
- Check outdoor lighting by the entrance and paths; the dark season is when you need it.

## Heating, ventilation and fire safety

- Service the heating system before the cold arrives — boiler, heat pump or district heating unit, following the manufacturer's interval.
- Bleed radiators that are cold at the top, and check the system pressure afterwards.
- Clean or replace ventilation filters so the house can breathe while windows stay closed. See how often to replace filters.
- Book the chimney sweep if you have a fireplace or wood stove, and check that the damper closes.
- Test smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms, and replace batteries if they are due.
- Check the fire extinguisher and fire blanket — the gauge should be in the green.

## Inside

- Check window and door seals for draughts — a strip of weatherstripping is cheap compared to the heating bill.
- Look for damp in the basement, attic and behind furniture on outside walls, where condensation shows up first when it gets cold.
- Clean the kitchen extractor filter and the dishwasher filter.
- Find the main water stopcock and make sure everyone at home knows where it is.
