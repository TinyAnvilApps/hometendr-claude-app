# HomeTendr

Your HomeTendr home in Claude: see what needs doing today and tick tasks off, look up items and warranties, check the house's health, plan the week, get an autumn or spring maintenance plan, register an appliance from a receipt or type plate, and put together a handover when you sell — with the same permissions as in the app.

## What's inside

- **MCP connector** (`.mcp.json`): `https://mcp.hometendr.com`. You sign in with your HomeTendr account (email and password, Apple or Google) and choose which homes to share; owners and admins decide in the HomeTendr app (Settings → AI) what AI apps may change.
- **Skills** (`skills/`): seasonal maintenance, register an item from a receipt or type plate, weekly plan, and a handover for selling the house.

Reading works on every HomeTendr plan; creating and changing things needs Pro or Max. Support: https://hometendr.com/support · Privacy: https://hometendr.com/privacy · Terms: https://hometendr.com/terms
